About Me

From visual design to software engineering

My journey in technology began with visual design. I started by exploring graphic design with tools like Photoshop and Illustrator, which gradually led me toward UI/UX design and Figma.

While working on different design projects, I became increasingly interested in understanding how the products I designed were actually built. That curiosity led me to Software Engineering, where I began exploring programming, databases, software design, and web development.

Today, I'm particularly interested in the intersection of software engineering, AI, automation, and user-centered design. I'm still learning and experimenting, but every project gives me an opportunity to turn what I learn into something practical.
